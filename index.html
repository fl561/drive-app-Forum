FAQs

Is my identity really hidden?
Yes. We never store your name, address, or contact details after delivery. All communications are encrypted and auto‑deleted within 24 hours.
How do I know my package is secure?
We use end‑to‑end encryption for all messages and photo proof. Your package is handled by vetted couriers with strict confidentiality agreements.
What areas do you serve?
Currently South Florida – ZIPs 33426, 33435, 33436, 33437. Expanding soon.
How much does it cost?
$5 flat fee per delivery – no hidden charges, no subscription.
What is contactless delivery?
Zero physical contact. We coordinate drop‑off locations, take encrypted photo proof, and all communication is ephemeral.
Do you offer tracking?
We provide real‑time updates via encrypted messages, but no permanent tracking logs are kept.
How is payment handled?
Payments appear as a generic service charge on your statement – no mention of Fed Jumper or delivery contents.
What is your refund policy?
Delivery fee is non‑refundable once pickup is completed. If we fail to deliver, we refund in full.
